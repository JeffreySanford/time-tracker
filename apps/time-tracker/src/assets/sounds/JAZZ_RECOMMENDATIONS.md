## Quick DIY Solution

If you have GarageBand (Mac) or Audacity (Free):

1. **Simple Jazz Loop Recipe:**
   - Soft piano playing basic jazz chords (Cmaj7, Am7, Dm7, G7)
   - Light brush drums at 70-80 BPM
   - Maybe subtle upright bass
   - Keep volume low (-20dB to -15dB)
   - Export as MP3, 128kbps

2. **Atmosphere Elements:**
   - Very quiet coffee shop ambience
   - Soft paper rustling
   - Distant conversations (barely audible)

## Specifications Reminder

- **File name**: `ambient.mp3` (exactly)
- **Location**: `src/assets/sounds/typewriter/ambient.mp3`
- **Duration**: 30-60 seconds (will loop)
- **Style**: Cool jazz, not energetic - think late night cafe
- **Volume**: Subtle enough to not distract from work
